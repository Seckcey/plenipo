# v1.17.0 — Paid AI keys, fenced by spending caps (Phase 16, Wave 3)

Plenipo can now pay for AI work with **your own keys**: OpenRouter, or an AI company's own service.
It spends nothing until you switch it on, set a monthly spending cap, and add a key. It never starts
a paid task that could go past a cap, and it records every paid task. Plenipo by **8 West Ventures,
LLC**.

This is the third of four waves of Phase 16. It was built beside Phase 21 (the workspace). Out of
the box, nothing you rely on changed: Plenipo still uses only your subscriptions.

## Highlights

- **Spending caps** (Settings → **Spending caps**): a monthly amount for the whole business, a
  department, or one position. Set the business's cap first; no paid key works without it. Every
  paid task counts against its position, its department, and the business, and the smallest amount
  left decides. The month starts over on the 1st at midnight, Pacific time.
- **The hard stop never goes over.** Before a paid task starts, Plenipo sets aside the most it could
  cost. If that does not fit under every cap, the task does not start, and the reason names the
  cap. At 80% of a cap you get a warning; when a cap stops paid work, you are told, once a month,
  with a notice and a banner on every page.
- **A record of every paid task**: what it cost, against which caps, and which key, by its name. A
  task whose bill cannot be read counts at the most it could have cost, never as zero.
- **The switch.** Settings → Switches → **Let workers use paid AI keys**, off to start with.
- **Your keys, typed only into Plenipo.** Each paid AI tool has a card on the AI tools page with
  **Save and check**, **Replace key**, and **Remove key**. Plenipo checks a key with the company,
  keeps it in Windows Credential Manager, and never shows it again.
- **OpenRouter**: one key for hundreds of models (Qwen, Mistral, Llama, Kimi K3, and more), with
  prices read from OpenRouter's own list before each task.
- **Each AI company's own service**: Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, Moonshot AI (Kimi), Google, DeepSeek,
  Z.ai, MiniMax, Mistral, and Alibaba Cloud, each with a card named by the company and its models
  priced from the company's own pages (September 30, 2026).
- **Paid models run only where you put them.** Add a paid model to a position's list (Settings → AI
  models), for example after Kimi on Kimi Code. The Router moves to it only when the routes before it
  are usage-limited or unavailable, and its reason says "It costs money".
- **The same model, more than one way.** Kimi K3 on Kimi Code, Ollama, OpenRouter, and Moonshot AI
  shows as one model; each card says where else it runs.

## Safer than before

- A key reaches only its own company, on the helper's first line of input: never on a command line,
  in a setting, in a log, or in a task's activity. Guard allows each paid AI tool one fixed address.
- Each request tells OpenRouter the most it may charge, so it goes nowhere dearer; models with fees
  beyond their token prices are not used.
- Where a company does not limit a model's thinking (xAI) or does not say whether it does (Moonshot
  AI, Z.ai), each step sets aside the most the model can write, and frees the rest when it ends.
- A removed key is erased from Windows Credential Manager first; a key the Vault will not let go of
  stays listed, so Remove can try again.

## Known limits

- **Paid workers answer in text only.** They cannot read files, run programs, or open web pages yet.
- **Paid keys work in your first organization only** for now; every organization keeps its own
  switch and spending caps.
- **Prices are dated.** A company that changes a price makes Plenipo count more or less than it spent
  until an update checks it again. OpenRouter's prices are read before every task.
- **xAI's, Moonshot AI's, and Z.ai's models need more room under a cap**, because each step sets
  aside the most they can write. A later update narrows this once your keys show their thinking stays
  inside the limit.
- A Google key on Google's free tier lets Google use what it is sent to improve its products.
- Alibaba Cloud keys must be made in its international (Singapore) region.

## Not yet checked with a real key

Nothing here has met a company's real service yet. Each card says so until its key passes its
check. The checks with your keys for Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, and Moonshot AI come first, on your
Windows PC; the rest follow when you have their keys. The paid Ollama plan's models are checked once
that plan starts.
