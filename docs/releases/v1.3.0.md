# v1.3.0 — Plenipo's browser, and the screen, mouse, and keyboard (Phase 10)

Workers can now do tasks on websites that have no official connection, in Plenipo's own
browser, and, as a last resort, see your screen and use the mouse and keyboard. You stay in
charge the whole time:

- Anything that sends, buys, or signs in waits for you.
- A sign on every page says when a worker is in control, and one click stops it.
- Every step is in the Activity trail with a screenshot.

Every role also now knows its job.

## Highlights

- **Plenipo's browser, never yours.** Workers open websites in the Microsoft Edge or Google
  Chrome already on your computer, but with Plenipo's own profile. Your sign-ins, cookies, and
  saved passwords are never used, and Plenipo's profile never saves passwords.
- **Websites you allow.** **Settings → Permissions → Websites** has three settings:
  - **Allowed:** websites workers may open without asking.
  - **Blocked:** websites workers may never open.
  - **Other websites:** ask you the first time (the default), or blocked.

  Websites on your own network, such as your router, open only when you list them.
  LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok, and Amazon start out blocked because their terms
  forbid automated use.

- **Nothing is sent without you.** Submitting a form, buying, signing in, and sending anything
  always wait for your approval, even on allowed websites. That includes data a page's own
  script tries to send after a click. The approval card shows exactly what will happen, the
  page's address, and a screenshot of the page.
- **No passwords, no secrets, no CAPTCHAs.** Workers never type into password, one-time-code,
  or card fields, and never type a secret. They never try to get past a CAPTCHA or other site
  security. When a website needs you signed in, open **Plenipo's browser** from the Websites
  settings and sign in yourself; workers use that sign-in.
- **Screenshots in the Activity trail.** Every significant step takes a picture of the page or
  screen: opening, clicking, typing, and each approval. Pictures show in the trail (**Show
  screenshot**) and on approval cards. Workers get them too, with a description in words for
  AI models that cannot see images.
- **The sign, Take over, and Stop.** Whenever a worker uses the browser or the mouse and
  keyboard, a banner on every page names it, with its page and last action:
  - **Take over:** that worker stops, and the browser tab stays open for you. Clicking or
    typing in the worker's page does the same.
  - **Stop all:** all control halts at once. Those workers lose their permissions for the rest
    of their task, and no worker can take control again until you press **Allow again**. The
    Windows tray menu has the same Stop.
  - The worker's page shows a colored frame and label, so you can tell it apart from your own
    browsing.
- **The screen, mouse, and keyboard, as a last resort.** Give the **Screen, mouse, and
  keyboard** permission set to a role of your own only when no API, program, or website will
  do. A worker must say why it wants control, and you are asked every time. While it has
  control, a small window stays above all others with **Take over** and **Stop**, and moving
  your mouse takes control back.
- **A new role: Web Assistant.** It does tasks on websites you allow: it finds information,
  fills in forms, and checks orders and statuses. The **Researcher** now reads websites for
  real (open, read, screenshot) but never clicks or types.
- **Every role knows its job.** Each built-in role's workers are told four things: what they
  are responsible for, what they hand back, what they must not do, and when to ask their lead.
  They are also told what their permissions let them do. VPs and Managers know their job even
  before anyone reports to them. For your own roles, **New role** and **Edit role** take
  "What this role does" and the same four lists, in your words. Every role's instructions show
  in its details on the Organization map.
- **The Documentation Writer commits its work.** The built-in Writer permission set can now
  save to git (pushing still asks you).

## Upgrading from 1.2.0

There is no Ledger change. On first start, Plenipo stores the starting website lists and adds
the **Web assistant** and **Screen, mouse, and keyboard** permission sets. It also updates the
built-in **Writer** set (save to git) and **Researcher** set (visit websites) if you never
changed them. Sets you changed are left alone. The **Web Assistant** role is added
to the hire palette, and built-in roles get their working instructions.

## Known limits

- **Not yet checked with the real AI tools.** Phase 10 was verified end to end with stand-ins
  for Claude Code and Codex, a test website on this computer, and a real Chromium, on Linux. The
  check on Windows with Edge and the real AI tools is in the Phase 10 checklist. Anything
  it finds goes into a patch release.
- **Terms of use are yours to check.** Many websites forbid automated use, and Google and Bing
  forbid automated searches. Plenipo cannot judge a website's terms, and the starting blocked
  list is not complete.
- **Some requests are not held:**
  - An ordinary page load that changes something (a badly built "delete" link) is not held,
    unless its words look like sending or changing something.
  - Requests a page makes on its own between a worker's actions are not held. Forms a page
    sends by itself are stopped.
  - Content an allowed page embeds from another website (a map, a video) loads with it.
- **Screenshots cannot be blurred.** They show whatever was on the page or screen, and stay in
  Plenipo's data folder on this computer.
- **On the desktop, only your mouse is noticed**, not your keyboard. Use **Take over** or
  **Stop**.
- **Images and Codex or Grok:** whether Codex and Grok pass screenshots to their models as
  pictures is not yet checked. Every screenshot also comes with a description in words.
- **Ollama has no Plenipo tools**, so a Web Assistant or Researcher on Ollama cannot open
  websites. Its workers are told why. Choose Claude Code, Codex, or Grok for those roles in
  **Settings → AI models**.

## Install

Download `Plenipo_1.3.0_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It is a per-user install with no admin
rights needed. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn: choose
**More info → Run anyway**.
