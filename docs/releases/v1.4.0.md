# v1.4.0 — Switches in Settings, and workers that learn from their work

You decide which features workers may use, and how much they ask you, with a few switches in
**Settings → Switches**. Workers also now learn from their work: they write down short lessons,
you keep the good ones, and that role's later workers follow them.

## Highlights

- **Settings → Switches.** Every switch applies to every worker, whatever its permissions:

  | Switch                                          | Starts | What it does                                                                                              |
  | ----------------------------------------------- | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
  | Plenipo's browser                               | On     | Off: no worker opens any website. Turning it off stops any worker using it now.                           |
  | Screen, mouse, and keyboard                     | Off    | The last resort. On: workers you allow can see the screen and, after asking you each time, use the mouse. |
  | Worker learning                                 | On     | Off: no lessons are recorded or used.                                                                     |
  | Sending forms and messages (without asking)     | Off    | On: workers send on your **allowed** websites without asking you.                                         |
  | Buying and paying (without asking)              | Off    | On: workers buy on your **allowed** websites without asking you.                                          |
  | Signing in (without asking)                     | Off    | On: workers press Sign in on your **allowed** websites without asking you. You still type passwords.      |
  | Hand me checks that a person is using a website | On     | CAPTCHAs. On: the worker hands the check to you and waits. Off: the worker stops and tells you.           |
  | Screenshots in the Activity trail               | On     | Off: steps keep no picture. Approval cards always keep theirs.                                            |

- **"Without asking" has limits.** Off means workers ask you first, as before. On, it works
  only on websites on your **Allowed** list (Settings → Permissions → Websites), never on the
  screen and mouse, and never where you set the action to **Blocked**. A role you set to ask
  you still asks. Every step is still in the Activity trail.
- **CAPTCHAs are handed to you.** When a website shows a check that a person is using it, the
  worker asks you to solve it. Plenipo's browser comes to the front with a purple sign ("Please
  solve this check yourself, then press Approve in Plenipo"), and the worker waits. When you
  press **Approve**, it reads the page again and carries on. Workers never click, type, or
  retry in a CAPTCHA.
- **Workers learn from their work.** At the end of a task, a worker can write down up to three
  short lessons for the next worker in its role, for example "On this shop's website, the order
  number is on the Orders page". Then:
  - Each lesson waits for you under **Approvals → New lessons**. Edit its words if you like,
    then **Keep** or **Discard**. Nothing waits on your answer.
  - Kept lessons go into the instructions of that role's later workers (the newest 20).
  - A role's details on the Organization map show **What it has learned**, with **Remove** for
    each lesson, and a **Learn on its own** switch: on, its lessons are kept without asking.
  - Lessons from a task that used websites or your screen, itself or any worker it handed work
    to, **always ask you**, with a warning, so a website can't plant one.
  - Lessons never change permissions or approvals.
- **Fixed:** with sending set to **Blocked**, data a page tried to send after a click asked you
  instead of being refused.

## Upgrading from 1.3.0

The Ledger gets one new table (`lessons`, migration 7), added on first start. Your permissions,
website lists, and approvals are unchanged, and workers ask you for everything they asked
before.

**One change to check:** the **Screen, mouse, and keyboard** switch starts **off**. If you gave
a role of your own the "Screen, mouse, and keyboard" permission set in 1.3.0, turn the switch on
in **Settings → Switches**. No built-in role has it.

## Risks, said plainly

- **A website can trick a worker.** Web pages can contain words written to steer AI workers.
  With a "without asking" switch on, an allowed website could lead a worker to send, buy, or
  press Sign in when you did not intend it. Turn those switches on only for websites you trust,
  and keep **Buying and paying** off unless you need it.
- **Terms of use are yours to check.** Many websites forbid automated use. Handing CAPTCHAs to
  you keeps Plenipo from getting past them, but using a site with workers can still break its
  terms.
- **Lessons from files are not flagged.** Only lessons from tasks that used websites or the
  screen are forced to ask. A role that learns on its own can keep a poor lesson from other
  work; remove it from the role's details.

## Free and Pro

Worker learning will be part of the **Pro** edition once the Free and Pro split arrives
([ADR-021](https://github.com/Seckcey/plenipo/blob/main/docs/adr/ADR-021-editions-and-license.md),
Free and Pro editions). Until then it is unlocked, like everything else in v1.4.0. The switches are
safety controls and stay in the Free edition.

## Known limits

- **Not yet checked with the real AI tools on Windows.** Verified end to end with stand-in AI
  tools and a real Chromium on Linux. The Windows check is in the checklist.
- **A CAPTCHA waits as long as an approval does.** If you don't answer in time, the worker is
  told you did not solve it and stops.

## Install

Download `Plenipo_1.4.0_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It is a per-user install with no admin
rights needed. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn: choose
**More info → Run anyway**.
