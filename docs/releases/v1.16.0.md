# v1.16.0 — Your workspace: panels, files, and more than one organization (Phase 21)

Lay Plenipo out your way, open and edit your project files without leaving it, and run a second
organization — for example a client's — in its own window. Plenipo by **8 West Ventures, LLC**.

This is Phase 21. It was built beside Phase 16's second wave (v1.15.0). Nothing you rely on changed
how it works, and your first organization stays exactly where it was.

## Highlights

- **Panels you can move.** The **Terminal** and the new **Files** panel sit on the left, right, or
  bottom. Drag an edge to resize. Use a panel's menu (or drag its tab) to move it, or **Pop out**
  to put it in its own window — on a second screen, say. Drag a tab off Plenipo's window and it
  pops out right there. **Put back** brings it home. A popped-out terminal keeps running.
- **The layout is remembered,** pop-outs included, after a restart. **Reset layout** (in a panel's
  menu, or Settings → Personalization) starts over.
- **Files.** The Files panel shows each project's folder and working copies. Double-click a file to
  **Open in Plenipo**: code with colors for its language, pictures shown. **Save** (or Ctrl+S) is
  recorded in the Activity trail as yours. **Ctrl+Shift+E** shows or hides Files.
- **One writer at a time.** If a worker is writing in a working copy, its files open read-only and
  say who is writing, with **Wait** or **Stop the worker**. What the worker writes shows in the
  editor as it lands, without reopening the file.
- **Files on an objective.** Drop files on an objective's box, from Files or from File Explorer.
  Plenipo copies them for the objective's workers; your originals are never changed.
- **More than one organization.** The **Organizations** menu at the top makes a **New
  organization**: copy the setup of one you have (departments, roles, permissions, AI model
  choices — no work and no secrets), or start from scratch. Templates are coming later. **Switch
  to** shows another organization in this window; **Open in a new window** puts it beside this
  one. Both can work at the same time.
- **Settings → Organization** renames the organization a window shows, and lists all of yours:
  switch, open, **Archive organization**, **Bring back**, and **Delete for good** (which offers to
  save its experienced workers to your Workforce first).

## Safer than before

- **Organizations never mix.** Each has its own Ledger, backups, working copies, screenshots,
  website sign-ins, and secrets (kept in the Vault under its own name). A window can only reach its
  own organization's work, approvals, and secrets.
- **Only the folders Plenipo knows.** The editor opens and saves files in your project folders and
  working copies only, never anywhere else on the PC. Workers still go through Guard, as before.
- **Nothing is started from a file.** Programs and scripts open as text. **Open in another program**
  is never offered for them; **Show in folder** is.
- **A pop-out can do nothing on its own.** Its organization's window draws it and makes every call.
- **Files on an objective stay private and safe.** Blocked files (like `.env`) never go on an
  objective. The copies are kept out of git, so they are never committed or pushed.
- **A worker's change is never saved over.** If a file changed after you started editing it,
  **Save** asks first.

## Known limits

- Your first organization cannot be archived or deleted: it keeps your Workforce, your tile, and
  your choices for the PC.
- The file view does not make, rename, or delete files, and unsaved edits are lost when Plenipo
  quits.
- An AI tool's update waits for your first organization's workers only; while it runs, every
  organization's new work for that tool waits.
- Restoring any organization's backup restarts all of Plenipo; the other organizations' work stops
  the normal way first.
- The Organization page's details panel stays with that page.
- The Free edition's one-organization limit is written down but not enforced until licenses exist.
- A file with mixed line endings is saved with its main one everywhere. A file whose name or
  folder has a comma opens in Plenipo only.

## Not yet checked on a real Windows PC

- **Pop out** on Windows (WebView2): the terminal in its own window, on a second screen, and back.
- Dragging a panel's tab off Plenipo's window.
- Dropping files from File Explorer on an objective.
- **Show in folder** and **Open in another program** (File Explorer and Windows' own programs).
- A second organization in its own window, closing it, and archiving it.
- The uninstaller's "delete my data" forgetting a second organization's secrets.

## Install

Download `Plenipo_1.16.0_x64-setup.exe` below and run it, or let Plenipo install the update. It is
a per-user install with no admin rights needed, signed as **8 West Ventures, LLC**. This update does
not change the Ledger's layout.
