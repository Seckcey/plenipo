# v1.19.0 — Plenipo on your phone, part 14A: the sealed line and approvals (Phase 14)

Plenipo can now be used from your phone's web browser: see your work, answer approvals, and press
**Stop all**, while your PC stays in charge. This release has everything on your PC's side. It turns
on in an update once 8 West's relay is ready; until then **Settings → Switches → Use Plenipo from
another device** says **Coming soon**. Plenipo by **8 West Ventures, LLC**.

## Highlights

- **Turn it on at your PC.** Settings → Switches → **Use Plenipo from another device**. It is off to
  begin with, and it is part of Pro. Turning it off cuts every phone off at once.
- **Add a phone at your PC.** Settings → **Devices** → **Add a phone** shows a picture code (QR code)
  and a 16-letter typed code. It works once, for 10 minutes. Scan it with your phone's camera, or
  type it in Plenipo's page at `remote.getplenipo.com`. Your PC then asks **Is this your phone?**,
  and nothing is added until you click **Add**.
- **Your face, fingerprint, or passcode.** When you add it, the phone sets up a passkey for Plenipo.
  Your PC checks it each time the phone signs in. You stay signed in for 30 minutes after your last
  tap, and 12 hours at most.
- **On your phone:** Home, Approvals, Work (your organization, projects, workers, and tasks),
  Activity, and More (the AI tools, Diagnostics, and lessons waiting), in the light or dark theme.
  **Approve** and **Refuse** work from the phone, and so does **Stop all**.
- **The first answer counts.** If an approval is answered on the PC and on the phone at the same
  moment, one answer wins, and the other is told it was already answered.
- **Keep some approvals on your PC.** Settings → Devices → **Approvals** lets you tick kinds of
  approvals (money, DNS, production servers, or every approval) that the phone shows with "Approve on
  your PC" and no buttons. None are ticked to begin with.
- **Your phones, listed.** Settings → Devices lists each phone, when it was added and last used, and
  whether it is signed in, with **Rename** and **Remove**. A removed phone is cut off at once.

## Safe as before

- **Sealed end to end.** The phone and your PC talk through 8 West's relay with a lock only they hold
  (Noise). The relay cannot read, change, copy, or make up a request. A request is never run twice.
- **Guard decides every request from a phone**, from a fixed list. A phone can never reach the
  terminal, files, the screen, Plenipo's browser, or your secrets, and can never change permissions,
  switches, Guard's rules, or the list of approvals kept on your PC.
- **Activity shows every request with the phone that sent it**: "Frank's phone asked to approve",
  and "Approved: git push origin (from Frank's phone)".
- **Wrong tries are stopped.** A pairing code stops working after 3 wrong tries, and wrong codes pause
  **Add a phone** for 15 minutes. A phone that fails its face, fingerprint, or passcode check 3 times
  is paused until you un-pause it on your PC. Someone who keeps trying to reach your PC as a phone
  stops it answering new phones for a while.
- **When your PC can't be reached**, the phone says so, and changes nothing. Nothing waits at the
  relay to run later.

## Good to know

- **What Plenipo sends to the relay**, and nothing else: from your PC, its relay key, its proof that
  it holds it, 8 West's signed weekly answer, the relay ID of each phone you remove, and sealed
  messages; from your phone, its pass and sealed messages. See [Free and Pro](../editions.md).
- **A Free copy never connects to the relay.** When Pro ends, phone access pauses, and your phones
  stay on the list for when Pro is back.
- **On an iPhone**, add the page to your Home Screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen), open it from
  there, and pair from inside it.
