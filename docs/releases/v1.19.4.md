# v1.19.4 — Plenipo on your phone is on

**Plenipo on your phone works now.** 8 West's relay is running at `relay.getplenipo.com`, and it
was checked from outside before this release. So the switch that said **Coming soon** now says
**Use Plenipo from another device**, and you can add your phone. Plenipo by **8 West Ventures,
LLC**.

## Highlights

- **Turn it on.** On your PC, open **Settings → Switches → Use Plenipo from another device**. Then
  open **Settings → Devices → Add a phone**. Your PC shows a picture code (QR code) and a typed
  code. Scan it with your phone's camera, and your phone opens Plenipo's page at
  `remote.getplenipo.com`. Your phone signs in with its face, fingerprint, or passcode.
- **What your phone can do.** Read every page, answer approvals, stop or allow work, give an
  objective, and get a notice when something needs you, even when the page is closed.
- **Sealed end to end.** Every message between your phone and your PC is sealed before it leaves.
  The relay in the middle passes it along and cannot read or change it. Your PC stays in charge:
  Guard decides every request, and Activity shows each one with the phone that sent it.
- **Part of Pro.** Phone access needs Pro. On Free, the switch says **Part of Pro**.

## For you, the owner

- Install this update on your PC. Plenipo offers it by itself (it checks GitHub once a day), or
  you can download it from this release.
- Then try it on a real phone, the checks only you can do: pair an iPhone from its Home Screen
  page and an Android phone from Chrome, sign in, approve and refuse something, and turn on notices
  on each. The list is in the
  [Phase 14 acceptance report](../phases/phase-14-acceptance-report.md).

## Good to know

- One small fix in the relay. When it ends a connection (to turn away a phone that sends too
  fast, say), it now waits for the other side's goodbye before it hangs up, as the WebSocket
  standard asks. Before, hanging up early could make the computer reset the connection, and on
  Windows a reset could wipe out the relay's last message, the reason it ended.
- Nothing else changed in this release.
- The relay is Plenipo's own program, from this repository (ADR-149, Plenipo runs its own relay).
  8 West's server installs each new release's relay by itself.
- What Plenipo sends the relay, and what the relay can and cannot see, is in
  [Free and Pro](../editions.md).
