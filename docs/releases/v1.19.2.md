# v1.19.2 — Plenipo on your phone, part 14C: notices when the page is closed (Phase 14 delivered)

Your phone now gets a **notice** when something needs you, even when Plenipo's page is closed. The
notice is sealed so only your phone can read it. This finishes Phase 14, Plenipo on your phone. Like
parts 14A and 14B, it reaches your phone once 8 West's relay is ready. Plenipo by **8 West Ventures,
LLC**.

## Highlights

- **Notices on this phone.** On your phone, open Plenipo, then **More → Notices on this phone**. Your
  PC then sends that phone the same notices it shows you: an approval waiting, a check to solve, a
  problem, finished work, a lesson, Plenipo itself, and spending.
- **Answer from the notice.** On Android, an approval's notice has **Approve** and **Refuse**, and a
  lesson's has **Keep** and **Discard**. **Refuse** and **Discard** answer right from the notice.
  **Approve** and **Keep** open Plenipo on that item: your phone unlocks first, and asks you to sign
  in if needed, then one tap answers it. A notice never approves anything by itself.
- **iPhone.** A tap on the notice opens that approval. On an iPhone, notices come only to Plenipo on
  your Home Screen, and the page shows how to add it there.
- **Your lock screen, your choice.** **On the lock screen**: show what it is, or show only
  "**Something needs you**". The choice stays on your phone, and applies before anything shows.
- **An old notice cannot fool you.** Opening a notice shows the item as it is now. If it was already
  answered, on your PC or on another phone, the phone says **Already answered**. A notice sent again
  by mistake shows once.
- **On your PC:** Settings → Notifications → **Notices on my phones** turns them off for every phone
  at once. The kinds you turn off there are off on your phones too.
- **Activity in plain words.** The phone's Activity says what happened in plain words. Everything
  else is still in Activity on your PC, under **All events**.

## Safe as before

- **Sealed for your phone alone.** Your PC seals each notice with your phone's own keys (the web's
  standard sealing) and signs it with its own notice key. The notice service (Apple's, Google's,
  Mozilla's, or Microsoft's) carries it and cannot read it. 8 West and its relay never see notices
  at all.
- **Guard decides where a notice goes:** only to a phone's own notice service, only on Pro, with
  phone access on.
- **Activity records each notice** with the phone and its kind, never what it said.
