# v1.26.0 — A chat window for each agent

**This release lets you pop out each agent's chat into a window of its own, so you can watch
several agents at once. Chats also remember their steps after Plenipo starts again.** Plenipo by
**8 West Ventures, LLC**.

## Highlights

- **Each agent's chat in a window of its own.** Pop out an agent's chat from its details on the
  map, its page, the Chat panel, or its live conversation, or double-click the agent on the map.
  The window is the same live chat, message box and all, and its title shows the agent's name. Up
  to six at once; **Put back**, or close the window, to bring it back to the Chat panel. The
  windows open again after a restart.
- **Chats remember their steps.** After Plenipo starts again, an agent's recent conversations show
  their steps and notes again, not only what was asked and answered.

## Fixed

- **An answer said once.** Opening the chat of an agent that had just finished no longer shows its
  answer twice.

## For you, the owner

- Install this update on your PC. Plenipo offers it by itself (it checks GitHub once a day), or
  you can download it from this release.
