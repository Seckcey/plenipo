# v1.21.0 — Mac and Linux: the shared base is ready

**This release finishes the groundwork for Plenipo on a Mac and Linux** (Phase 23, Wave 1). There
is still no Mac or Linux download: Linux comes next, then the Mac. On Windows you will notice three
small changes in wording, and Guard is a little stricter. Plenipo by **8 West Ventures, LLC**.

## Highlights

- **Each system speaks its own words.** On a Mac, Plenipo will say "this Mac", "the menu bar", and
  "your Mac's Keychain", and show shortcuts as ⌘S; on Linux, "this computer" and "your file
  manager" (ADR-155). Windows keeps its words.
- **The terminal on a Mac and Linux** offers your own shell, zsh, bash, and fish, and closing a
  terminal ends the programs started in it, as on Windows.
- **Guard's safety review for a Mac and Linux** found six things and fixed all of them
  ([the review](../phases/phase-23-wave-1-guard-review.md)):
  - On a Mac and Linux, Stop now stops everything a worker started, even a program that stepped
    out of the worker's group (ADR-158, a program that leaves its group still ends with its work).
  - More of a Mac's and Linux's own tools are blocked, such as ones that type for you, take a
    picture of the screen, or read the clipboard.
  - Plenipo checks a file's path again right before it reads or writes the file.
- **Updates know which system they are on,** and only one Plenipo runs at a time on every system.

## For you, the owner

- Install this update on your PC. Plenipo offers it by itself (it checks GitHub once a day), or
  you can download it from this release.
- Nothing to check by hand for this one: Windows works as before.

## Good to know

- **Three sentences read a little differently on Windows:** "Pop-up notices from Windows wait
  while this is on…" (Do not disturb), "Set the right date and time on this PC" (License), and
  "This PC stays in charge" (Devices).
- **"Open in another program" refuses a few more kinds of files,** whatever system you are on:
  Ruby, PHP, and Lua scripts, and installer and disk files such as `.bin`, `.deb`, and `.dmg`. They
  open in Plenipo instead.
- **New installs block more risky programs**, most of which only exist on a Mac or Linux. Your
  own lists do not change.
- **On every system, a copy or move into a folder that is really a link leading outside the
  project counts as outside,** so it asks you first.
- The plan, every decision, and what is left are in the
  [Phase 23 checklist](../phases/phase-23-checklist.md). Community (Phase 24) is being built beside
  it (ADR-160).
