# v1.10.0 — You control how every worker works (Phase 17)

You can now decide how each of your agents works, at the level that fits: the whole
organization, a department, a role, or one agent. You can give an agent a specialty, turn its
learning on or off, and archive it, bring it back, or delete it for good. Agents you want to keep
go to your new **Workforce**, to hire again later. Every option in an agent's details now says,
in one line, what it does. And Plenipo sends shorter instructions on routine tasks.

Nothing you rely on changed how it works. Your organization, projects, workers, approvals,
permissions, servers, switches, and terminal are where you left them.

## Highlights

- **Model and effort rules, in layers.** Settings → AI models → **Model and effort rules** has a
  rule for the whole organization, one for each department, each role's model choices (as
  before), and one for any agent you give its own. Each rule can list models in order, the effort
  for each, an **effort for any other model**, and **AI companies never to use**.
  - **The closest rule wins:** an agent's own rule, then its role's, then its department's, then
    the organization's.
  - **"Never" means never:** AI companies you never want add up across the rules.
  - The reason Plenipo gives for a model now says which rule decided: "It runs at high effort,
    from the Development department's rule."
- **Effort for one agent.** An agent's **AI model** tab has an **Effort** choice. Changing only
  the effort never hires a new agent: its open conversation uses the new effort from its next
  task. (Changing the AI tool or model still hires a new agent, and says so first.)
- **Specialties.** A role can have specialties, like a Senior Developer who works on databases.
  A specialty adds its own lines to the role's working instructions and suggests models and
  permissions (suggestions never change anything on their own). Built in: Senior Developer (7),
  Designer (3), Security Auditor (2), Operations Engineer (4), Researcher (2), and
  Documentation Writer (2). You can add your own to any role. Hire with a specialty, and the Hire
  box suggests a title ("Database Developer").
- **Learning, in layers.** Worker learning (Settings → Switches) is still the main switch. Under
  it, each role and each agent can learn or not; the agent's own setting wins, then its role's.
- **Archive, bring back, and delete for good**, for agents, projects, and now departments too.
  The Organization page's **List** view has an **Archived** tab.
  - **Bring back** puts it back as it was, with its settings. What was archived together comes
    back together.
  - **Delete for good** asks first, names everything that goes, and says it cannot be undone. It
    is refused while anything still has work to finish. A short record stays in the Ledger, so
    older work still shows who did it. Nothing on your PC is deleted.
  - **Archive department** replaces Remove department.
- **Experience and your Workforce.** Every agent has an **Experience** score: 10 for each lesson
  it wrote that you kept, and 1 for each task it finished. When you delete for good, Plenipo
  lists the agents that go and checks the ones above your organization's average, to save them
  to your **Workforce**. The List view's **Workforce** tab shows the agents you saved, with their
  experience and where they worked. **Hire into a team** brings one back with its settings,
  experience, and lessons; the Hire box offers them too.
- **The details panel, rebuilt.** An agent's details now have six tabs: **Overview**, **Job**,
  **AI model**, **Work**, **Team**, and **Manage**. Every option has a line under it saying what
  it does. The Work tab shows its permissions and its project's limit. Drag the panel's left edge
  (or use the arrow keys on it) to make it wider; Plenipo remembers the width.
- **Shorter instructions on routine tasks.** Plenipo now measures how much it sends with each
  task and records it in the Ledger. A conversation gets the full instructions when it starts,
  when the AI tool says it shortened its memory, after several objectives, and for a large job;
  other tasks get a short reminder. Handoffs use a short, labeled format in plain words.
  (Measured numbers: see the acceptance report.)

## Decisions

- [ADR-041: model, effort, and learning in layers](https://github.com/Seckcey/plenipo/blob/main/docs/adr/ADR-041-model-effort-learning-layers.md)
  — **accepted** by you, with your choice that AI companies never to use add up across the
  rules. Accepting it means the closest rule wins for models and effort, and the reason for each
  model names the rule that decided.
- [ADR-042: specialties](https://github.com/Seckcey/plenipo/blob/main/docs/adr/ADR-042-specialties.md)
  — **accepted**. Accepting it means specialties are data under each role, like roles themselves,
  and a specialty only adds lines and suggestions; lessons stay with the role.
- [ADR-043: archive, bring back, and delete for good](https://github.com/Seckcey/plenipo/blob/main/docs/adr/ADR-043-archive-bring-back-delete.md)
  — **accepted**, with your choice that deleting a project or department takes along what was
  archived with it. Accepting it means a deleted item keeps a short record in the Ledger and
  never deletes files on your PC.
- [ADR-044: prompts sized to the job](https://github.com/Seckcey/plenipo/blob/main/docs/adr/ADR-044-prompts-sized-to-the-job.md)
  — **accepted**. Accepting it means Plenipo measures what it sends, sends the full instructions
  only when they are needed, and never uses a private shorthand.
- [ADR-045: experience and the Workforce](https://github.com/Seckcey/plenipo/blob/main/docs/adr/ADR-045-experience-and-the-workforce.md)
  — **your addition, accepted as written**, with experienced agents checked by default.

## Upgrading from 1.9.0

Run the 1.10.0 installer, or choose **Install now** when Plenipo offers the update. The first time
1.10.0 opens your Ledger, it backs it up ("Before a new version") and then updates its layout (to
layout 9: specialties, archive and delete records, and the Workforce). Your organization, work,
and settings carry over; nothing needs setting up.

**Going back to 1.9.0**, if you ever need to: 1.9.0 cannot read the new layout, so it runs on a
temporary Ledger and leaves yours untouched. To go back for good, restore the backup made
"Before a new version" from Diagnostics in 1.9.0 (anything done in 1.10.0 after that backup is not
in it). Going forward to 1.10.0 again finds your Ledger as 1.10.0 left it.

## Known limits and risks

- **Not yet checked on Windows by you.** Please try the acceptance walk-through on your PC with
  your real AI tools: a rule for the organization, one department, and one agent; learning off
  for one agent; a Database developer; archive, bring back, archive, and delete for good (saving
  one agent to your Workforce and hiring it again); and archiving a department.
- **"Shortened its memory" depends on each AI tool saying so.** Claude Code and the tools that
  speak ACP (Kimi, Grok) report it; for others, Plenipo relies on the other rules (a new
  conversation, several objectives, a large job).
- **Authorized penetration testing is not a built-in Security Auditor specialty** (the plan listed
  it). You can add it yourself as your own specialty; Guard still decides what that worker may do.
- **The canvas's trash can and Archived drawer** come with Phase 18; for now, the Archived list is
  in the List view.
