# v1.11.0 — The organization canvas (Phase 18)

The Organization page's canvas is now the easiest way to run your organization. Put every tile
where you want it, and Plenipo remembers. Rewire who reports to whom by dragging the end of a
line. Lend an agent to another team for one objective, and it comes home by itself. Drop an agent
on the trash can to archive it, with Undo. See at a glance who is working, hand-offs moving
along the lines, where each worker's work runs, and what it is touching. And **Watch** a worker
write code as it happens, line by line. Your own tile shows your picture, status, mood, and
message.

Nothing you rely on changed how it works. Your organization, projects, workers, approvals,
permissions, servers, switches, and terminal are where you left them.

## Highlights

- **A toolbar on the canvas:** **Select**, **Move the view**, and **Arrange** (keys V, H, and A;
  hold the space bar to move the view), **Tidy up**, zoom, **Fit to screen**, **Filters**,
  **Legend**, **Where**, **Oversight lines**, **Add** (a department, a project, a new role, or hire
  an agent), the **trash can**, and **?** (a guide to the canvas).
- **Arrange freely.** Drag any tile to an empty spot and it stays there, even after a restart.
  A tile moves with its team; hold **Alt** to move it alone. With a tile selected, **Alt + the
  arrow keys** move it. A new hire appears beside its lead, wherever you put the lead. **Tidy
  up** puts everything back in neat rows, with **Undo**.
- **Rewire by dragging a line's end.** Select an agent to see the round ends of its lines. Drag
  the end of its "reports to" line onto another agent to move it there, or an oversight line's
  end onto another team (or another reviewer). The same rules apply as before, and a drop the
  rules refuse says why. With the keyboard, press Enter on a line end to choose from a list.
- **Move or lend.** Drop an agent on another team's lead: **Move here** (for good), **Lend for
  one objective**, or **Lend until I send it home**. Only on-call agents are lent (every built-in
  worker role). While lent, it works for that team, under that team's project's permission
  limit, working copy, and AI tools. A **Lent** badge and a dashed line show where it is; **Send
  home** is on the badge and in its Team tab. Lent for one objective, it comes home by itself
  when that objective is done.
- **The trash can.** Drop an agent on it to archive it, with **Undo**. Drop a Supervisor or a
  Manager, and Plenipo first asks to archive its project or department. Click the trash can for
  the **Archived** drawer: bring things back, or delete them for good (Phase 17's list, with the
  same questions).
- **Filters and the legend.** Show only the agents you want, by department, project, status, AI
  tool, AI company, rank, or specialty; the leads above them stay, faded, so the lines still make
  sense. The **Legend** explains every mark on the canvas, and Plenipo remembers whether you
  showed it.
- **The live view.** Hand-offs move along the lines from the agent that asked to the agent that
  took the work (still arrows when Windows' animation effects are off). Turn on **Where** to see,
  under each working tile, where its model thinks ("Thinks in Anthropic's cloud"), where its work
  runs (this PC, or a server by name), and what it is touching (a folder in its working copy, a
  server, a website, or the screen). It reads only what Plenipo already records.
- **Watch a worker write code.** **Watch** on a working agent (on the canvas, in its details, or
  in the terminal panel's **New** menu) opens a read-only tab at the bottom: each file its worker
  changed in this objective, and the file itself with **new** and **changed** lines marked. With
  Claude Code and Kimi, you see the code appear as it is written, marked **being written — not
  saved yet**, then **saved**, or **refused** if Guard said no. **Follow along**, or **Pin this
  file**. **Stop** stops the worker. It shows only what Guard lets that worker change: never a
  blocked file, never a secret. Large files and pictures show a short summary.
- **Your tile.** Your picture, a status light (**Available**, **Busy**, **Away**, **Do not
  disturb**), a mood, and a short message, on the canvas and in a new button in the top bar. You
  choose the picture with Windows' own "Open" box; Plenipo keeps a small copy on this PC and never
  sends it anywhere. While you are on **Do not disturb**, Windows pop-up notices wait and come
  as one when you turn it off; the bell still counts them.

## Safer than before

- **A moved agent works under its new project's limits.** While checking the code for lending,
  we found that a full-time agent moved to another project kept its old project's permission
  limit and working copy until it started a new conversation. Plenipo now reads each task's own
  team, so a moved agent works under its new project's limits from its next objective.
- **A refused edit's record keeps none of its text.** When Guard refuses a change to a file, the
  record in the Ledger keeps the file, the size, and why — no longer up to 200 characters of the
  refused text.

## Decisions

- [ADR-053: the organization canvas](https://github.com/Seckcey/plenipo/blob/main/docs/adr/ADR-053-the-organization-canvas.md)
  — **accepted**. Accepting it means tiles move with their team (Alt: one
  tile), dropping on an empty spot places a tile, moving tiles is not listed in Activity,
  dropping a lead on the trash can asks to archive its project or department, and filters hide
  what does not match.
- [ADR-054: move or lend](https://github.com/Seckcey/plenipo/blob/main/docs/adr/ADR-054-move-or-lend.md)
  — **accepted**. Accepting it means only on-call agents are lent, "one
  objective" is the borrowing team's whole objective, the borrowing team decides the limits
  while it helps, Send home lets it finish its task, and Guard reads each task's own team.
- [ADR-055: Watch](https://github.com/Seckcey/plenipo/blob/main/docs/adr/ADR-055-watch-a-worker-write-code.md)
  — **accepted**: a refused edit's record
  keeps no text. Accepting it means one Watch tab per agent, changes waiting for your approval
  shown, and the lines kept only in memory while Plenipo runs.
- [ADR-056: the owner's tile](https://github.com/Seckcey/plenipo/blob/main/docs/adr/ADR-056-the-owners-tile.md)
  — **accepted**. Accepting it means Do not disturb holds Windows notices and your
  tile is kept in the Ledger.

## Upgrading from 1.10.0

Run the 1.11.0 installer, or choose **Install now** when Plenipo offers the update. The first time
1.11.0 opens your Ledger, it backs it up ("Before a new version") and then updates its layout (to
layout 11: where you put each tile, and lent agents). Your organization, work, and settings carry
over; the canvas starts in its automatic layout.

**Going back to 1.10.0**, if you ever need to: 1.10.0 cannot read the new layout, so it runs on a
temporary Ledger and leaves yours untouched. To go back for good, restore the backup made
"Before a new version" from Diagnostics in 1.10.0 (anything done in 1.11.0 after that backup is not
in it).

## Known limits and risks

- **Not yet checked on a real Windows PC.** Still to walk through with real AI
  tools: arrange a few tiles and restart; rewire two lines; lend a Security Auditor to another
  department for one objective and see it come back; the trash can and the drawer; a filter;
  Where; and Watch with a Senior Developer on Claude Code.
- **Letter by letter depends on the AI tool.** Claude Code and Kimi stream a file change while
  they write it (Kimi's was checked against its recorded real program). Codex sends each change
  whole, so Watch shows it when it is saved. Grok's streaming has no recording yet; it shows each
  change when saved until checked. Ollama's models do not change files.
- **After a restart, Watch lists the files changed** in an agent's latest objective from the
  Ledger, without their lines (the lines are kept only while Plenipo runs).
- **Your picture and profile stay on this PC.** Sharing them with other people comes with
  Phase 24.
