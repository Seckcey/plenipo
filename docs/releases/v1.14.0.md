# v1.14.0 — Who made each model, and Antigravity from Google (Phase 16, Wave 1)

Every model Plenipo knows now says **who made it**, apart from the **AI tool** that runs it. A
review that must come from a different AI company now compares the companies that made the
models, so an OpenAI model on Ollama no longer counts as "a different company" than Codex. Your
list of models can be grouped by who made them or by AI tool. And **Antigravity**, Google's own AI
tool, joins as the sixth AI tool, so Gemini models work with your Google sign-in.

This is the first of four waves of Phase 16. It was built beside Phase 20's Slack and Google
connections. Nothing you rely on changed how it works.

## Highlights

- **Who made it.** Settings → AI models → **Your models** has a **Who made it** column, and
  **Group by: Who made it / AI tool**. Both show exactly the same models. Plenipo remembers your
  choice on this PC.
- **Cross-company review by who made the model.** "Prefer" or "require a different AI company"
  compares who made the model doing the review with who made the model whose work it reviews.
  Kimi K3 on Kimi and Kimi K3 on Ollama are one company; DeepSeek and Z.ai inside Ollama are two.
  The reason on screen names the companies.
- **AI companies never to use** now also skips a company's models on another AI tool ("never
  OpenAI" skips gpt-oss on Ollama), and the list offers the companies that make models (DeepSeek,
  Z.ai, MiniMax, NVIDIA, Google), not only the AI tools' own.
- **A model whose maker is not known plays safe.** It shows **Who made it: Not known**. A review
  that must come from a different company never uses it, a review that only prefers one tries it
  last, work it did never counts as coming from a different company, and a role with AI companies
  never to use skips it (it could be one of them).
- **Exact Claude versions.** The model menus say what each Claude name is now ("opus — now Opus
  5.5"), and the exact versions can be chosen too, so a role can stay on one version.
- **Codex's newest model** is in its list. OpenAI's older models are not: every one tried on a
  ChatGPT sign-in was refused.
- **Antigravity (Google's AI tool).** Install it with Google's installer, then choose **Sign in**
  on its card: it opens in a tab, you sign in with Google, then type `/exit`. It runs eleven Gemini
  models, two of Anthropic's, and one of OpenAI's, and its Models tab says who made each one.
  Workers on it answer in text only.
- **Every model list says who made each model:** each AI tool's Models tab ("made by OpenAI") and
  every model menu ("opus — now Opus 5.5 — made by Anthropic").

## Safer than before

- **Antigravity runs with a settings folder of its own,** in Plenipo's app data, written again
  before every run: paid AI credits off, strict permissions, and every kind of its own tool
  denied (programs, web pages, reading and writing files, add-ons). None of your own Antigravity
  settings, hooks, or add-ons apply to Plenipo's tasks. Your Google sign-in stays in Windows
  Credential Manager.
- **Plenipo checks that it held.** A task where Antigravity did not start with Plenipo's settings,
  or where one of its own tools ran anyway (or failed for any reason but a refused permission), is
  stopped.
- **No API keys.** Before every task Plenipo checks Antigravity's sign-in. It counts as your Google
  sign-in only when Antigravity offers a model Plenipo knows another company made; a Gemini API key
  (billed per use) is refused, and Plenipo never passes one.
- **No updates in the middle of a task.** Antigravity's own background updates are off in
  Plenipo's tasks; **Update** on its card updates it between tasks.

## Known limits

- Antigravity's own web search is not covered by any setting Plenipo found. If it runs, Plenipo
  stops the task, after the search.
- A task for Antigravity whose words start with `/` fails with Antigravity's own message.
- Anything set up inside Antigravity from its **Sign in** tab stays in Plenipo's settings folder
  for it.

## Not yet checked on a real Windows PC

- The grouped model list and the made-by words on the owner's own models.
