# v1.19.3 — Plenipo on your phone: the relay, built in this repository

Plenipo on your phone needs a **relay**: the small server in the middle that passes sealed
messages between your PC and your phones. From this release, the relay is **Plenipo's own**, built
in this repository and carried by each release, for 8 West to run on its server (ADR-149, Plenipo
runs its own relay). Nothing changes on your screen yet: the switch still says **Coming soon**
until 8 West has the relay running and checked, and the release after this one turns it on.
Plenipo by **8 West Ventures, LLC**.

## Highlights

- **Plenipo's own relay.** A small program (`plenipo-relay`, for Linux, about 2 MB) that does
  exactly what the written agreement between your PC and your phone says: it checks that a PC
  holds its key and is on Pro (with 8 West's signed weekly answer and 8 West's public keys), lets a
  phone through only with a pass its PC signed, and passes sealed messages along as they are. It
  keeps nothing and reads nothing.
- **Built for the internet.** Limits per internet address and per PC, a cap on connections, a
  budget of messages per minute, timeouts, an off switch, a health check, and logs that never hold
  a message, a key, a pass, or a code.
- **In the release.** `plenipo-relay-1.19.3-linux-x86_64` and its `.sha256` are attached to this
  release, next to the phone's page. 8 West's server fetches each new release's relay by itself,
  checks it, and steps back if anything is wrong, the way the phone page's server does.
- **Tested with Plenipo itself.** Plenipo's own PC side and a stand-in phone pair, sign in, and
  ask through the real relay in the tests, and the real app with a real browser as the phone runs
  against it too.

## For you, the owner

- Nothing to do on your PC. Phone access turns on in the next release, once the relay answers at
  `relay.getplenipo.com` and the repository variable `PLENIPO_RELAY_LIVE` is set.
- The relay's setup on 8 West's server, in plain words, is in `crates/relay/deploy/README.md`:
  look first, install, the proxy host, the Cloudflare record, and a check from outside.

## Good to know

- The relay's server address and sign-in are not in this repository, and never will be. Plenipo
  knows only the name `relay.getplenipo.com`.
- What Plenipo sends the relay, and what the relay can and cannot see, is in
  [Free and Pro](../editions.md).
- The decision and its reasons: ADR-149 in `docs/adr/`.
