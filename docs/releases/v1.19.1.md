# v1.19.1 — Plenipo on your phone, part 14B: everything else that is safe (Phase 14)

More of Plenipo now works from your phone's browser, with your PC still in charge. Like part 14A,
it reaches your phone once 8 West's relay is ready. Plenipo by **8 West Ventures, LLC**.

## Highlights

- **Allow again** after **Stop all**, right in the phone's top bar, the same as on your PC.
- **Stop the worker.** On the phone's Work page, a working task has **Stop the worker**. It asks
  first, then stops that task. What the worker already did stays.
- **Give objective.** A worker's page on the phone has a box to give it an objective, in words.
  Its worker starts at once, and the phone opens the conversation so you can follow it. Files stay
  on your PC.
- **Run again** and **Leave stopped.** When Plenipo closed unexpectedly on your PC, Home on the
  phone says so and lists the tasks that stopped with it. Each organization has its own notice.
  Nothing runs again until you choose.
- **Keep** or **Discard** a lesson a worker learned, as written. To change its words first, open it
  on your PC.
- **AI tools by name.** More → **AI tools** on the phone names each AI tool on your PC and says
  whether it can work now: **Ready**, or what it needs from you on your PC.
- **Activity without the pages you opened.** The phone's Activity shows what happened. The pages
  your phone opened are still recorded, and show in Activity on your PC.

## Safe as before

- Each of these goes through **Guard**, from the phone's fixed list, and **Activity names the
  phone** that asked ("Frank's phone asked to send an objective").
- A phone still can never reach the terminal, files, the screen, Plenipo's browser, or your
  secrets, and can never change permissions, switches, Guard's rules, or the approvals you keep on
  your PC.
- The phone's page is always the newest one. When your PC still runs v1.19.0, the page offers
  nothing that version cannot do.
