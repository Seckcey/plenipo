# v1.18.0 — Free, Pro, and Partner, and the license key (Phase 11A)

Plenipo now comes as **Free**, **Plenipo Pro**, and **Plenipo Partner**. Free is enough to run one
real project from start to finish, with every safety feature and every AI tool. Pro is for running a
business on it, and Partner is for companies that run Plenipo for their clients. Plenipo by **8 West Ventures, LLC**.

## Highlights

- **Free**: 1 organization, 1 department, 1 project, and 3 workers on the job at a time. Free has
  no card, no account, and no sign-up. **A Free copy never contacts 8 West.**
- **Plenipo Pro** ($19 a month, or $190 a year): up to 3 organizations, more departments and
  projects, 4 workers at a time in each organization, business departments, workers that learn
  from their work (lessons), Connections, and add-on tools.
- **Plenipo Partner**, for IT companies and agencies that run Plenipo for clients: everything in
  Pro, for 10 organizations ($79 a month), 25 ($149 a month), or any number ($299 a month), per
  technician. Yearly plans give 2 months free.
- **Settings → License.** Enter a license key and Pro turns on at once, with no restart. The screen
  shows:
  - Free or Pro, and why
  - the key's ID (never the key)
  - the plan, how many organizations it covers and how many are in use, and the paid-through date
  - when Plenipo last checked with 8 West, and when it checks next

  It has **Check now** and **Remove the key**. The key is kept in Windows Credential Manager.

- **The weekly check.** A Pro copy checks with 8 West about once a week. It sends exactly two
  things: the key's ID and the version of Plenipo. It never sends your projects, files, or work. A
  test checks the request byte for byte.
- **No internet is fine.** Pro keeps working for 30 days between checks, and a failed check never
  turns it off. The 30 days count from 8 West's own signed time, so changing the PC's clock does not
  change them.
- **When Pro ends, nothing is taken away.** Every organization, department, project, and record
  stays. Only making something new past a Free limit waits. Connections, add-on tools, and lessons
  pause; **Disconnect** always works; and everything comes back when Pro does, with no new sign-in.
- **A fourth worker on Free waits its turn.** Work handed to a fourth worker is never refused. It
  waits, with a plain note, and starts by itself when one finishes.
- **"Part of Plenipo Pro" notes.** Settings → Connections, worker learning, and your organizations
  say plainly what Pro adds. Each note has a button to Settings → License.

## Safe as before

- Safety is never part of Pro. Guard, permissions, approvals, the Vault, the Ledger, the Activity
  trail, spending caps, and every AI tool work the same on Free and Pro. A test runs the same work on
  both and finds the same tools, refusals, and records.
- Every license action is in the Activity trail, by the key's ID only.
- Uninstalling with "Also delete my Plenipo data" removes the license key too.

## Good to know

- One Pro or Partner license is for one person, on any of their own PCs. The number of
  organizations it covers is written in the key and counted on your PC; nothing is sent to 8 West.
- Keys come from 8 West's store at getplenipo.com, by email. Stripe sends the receipts.
- The account service that sells keys and answers the weekly check is run by 8 West Ventures, LLC,
  separately from the app.
