# v1.7.0 — A new look: one design system for every screen (Phase 12A)

Plenipo now looks like one tidy, dark control panel, like the UniFi network screens it was
modeled on. Every page is built from the same building blocks, so the pages that come next
(Phase 12: Home, a page for each department, project, worker, and task, and the terminal panel)
will look and work the same way.

Nothing you rely on changed how it works. Your organization, projects, workers, approvals,
permissions, servers, and switches are where you left them.

## Highlights

- **A new frame around every page.**
  - **The left strip:** each section's icon with its name under it (your choice), a blue marker on
    the page you are on, and counts for workers working, programs running, and requests waiting
    for you. Settings and Diagnostics sit at the bottom.
  - **The top bar:** **Showing** (all of your organization, a department, or a project: picking one
    opens it), the page's name, a sun/moon button for **light or dark**, and a **bell** with the
    number of requests waiting for your approval.
  - **Notices** above the page: the sign when a worker uses the browser or a server, approvals
    waiting, and Ledger notices, each with its button and **Dismiss**.
- **Smaller, tighter text** (13 points instead of 15, your choice), so more fits on screen.
- **A light theme.** Dark stays the default. The sun/moon button switches, and Plenipo remembers
  your choice.
- **Status always has a word.** Every status mark has its word next to it, and its own shape
  (● working, ▲ blocked, ◆ failed, ○ idle, ◐ waiting), so it reads without color.
- **The Gallery** (Diagnostics → **Open the gallery**) shows every building block, in both themes:
  status marks, the 24-hour activity strip, cards, a 5,000-row table with filters, the detail
  view with a timeline, the relationship map, notices, and controls. Its first section shows your
  real departments and projects with their activity from the last 24 hours.

## For the next pages

- **Activity strips come from the Ledger.** A new query counts what happened in 15-minute buckets
  for a department, a project, or a position, with problems in red and approvals in violet.
- **Big lists stay fast.** Tables and card grids draw only what is on screen: 5,000 rows scroll
  and sort smoothly.
- **No raw colors.** Every color comes from one list (the design tokens), and a check refuses any
  other color in the app's code.

## Decisions

- [ADR-030: one design system for every screen](https://github.com/Seckcey/plenipo/blob/main/docs/adr/ADR-030-design-system.md)
  — **accepted.** Every screen uses these building blocks; no screen gets its own one-off style.
- [ADR-031: the terminal panel](https://github.com/Seckcey/plenipo/blob/main/docs/adr/ADR-031-terminal-panel.md)
  — **proposed**, for Phase 12. It is not built in this release.

## Upgrading from 1.6.0

Plenipo adds one index to the Ledger (migration 8), which makes the activity strips fast. It
takes a backup first, as with every Ledger upgrade. Nothing else changes: the theme starts dark,
and **Showing** starts on your whole organization.

## Known limits

- **The pages themselves are the same pages, in the new look.** Home and the new department,
  project, worker, and task pages come in Phase 12.
- **Not yet checked on Windows by you.** Checked end to end on Linux with the real app, stand-in AI
  tools, and a synthetic SSH server. Windows builds and starts in CI. The Phase 11 Windows check
  against a real server is still to do
  ([Phase 11 checklist](https://github.com/Seckcey/plenipo/blob/main/docs/phases/phase-11-checklist.md)).
