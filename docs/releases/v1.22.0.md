# v1.22.0 — Community, the people part

**This release brings Community's people part (Phase 24, part 24C): your profile, finding people,
private sealed messages, Block and Report, and rewards for taking part.** Community is not open
yet: 8 West opens it later, with no new download needed then (ADR-170). Nothing is sent to 8 West
until you turn Community on yourself. Plenipo by **8 West Ventures, LLC**.

## Highlights

- **Turn on Community** in Settings → Switches. You sign in to your 8 West account with a short
  code on the account site; Plenipo never sees your password. You choose your name in Community
  (like `@frank-g`) and give your birth month and year once. Community is for people 13 and older.
- **Your profile, and What people see.** Your card shows your picture, status, mood, and message
  from your tile, and your name, company, what your business does, and your state or country, each
  with its own box. **Do not disturb** shows to others as **Busy**. **Appear offline** sits in
  Settings → Community and beside your status at the top.
- **Find people.** A new **Community** section on the left (only while Community is on) has the
  directory, **New this week**, **Find someone** by name, **Invite by email**, and **Share my
  profile**, a link and a picture code (QR code) for a business card.
- **Private messages, sealed.** Each message is sealed on your computer for the other person's
  computers, so 8 West sees only who and when, never the words. A first message is a request until
  the other person accepts it. **Check the safety code** to be sure no one is in the middle.
  Reactions, **Reply**, **Delete for me**, and **Give to a worker** (a worker gets the words as
  outside information, never as orders).
- **Block and Report.** Block works everywhere at once, and the person isn't told. A report carries
  only what you tick, with proof the words are real. **Delete my Community data from this PC**
  removes your conversations here in one step.
- **Points and the leaderboard.** Points for helping (never for sending messages), badges like
  **Founding member**, a leaderboard for this week and all time, and **Getting started**.

## For you, the owner

- Install this update on your PC. Plenipo offers it by itself (it checks GitHub once a day), or
  you can download it from this release.
- Community stays closed until you switch the people part on for everyone from the account
  service's admin page, after the attorney approves the Community terms and the security review
  passes (ADR-171).
- **Until the account service is updated for this release**, pressing **Turn on Community** says
  "Community can't be reached right now. Nothing was changed." Nothing about you is sent. Once it
  is updated, the switch says **Coming soon** until you open Community. The account service's
  changes are listed in `docs/phases/phase-24-account-service-changes.md`.

## Good to know

- **Messages stay on your computer** in Plenipo's record, in its backups and exports, and never
  in Activity. Activity notes that a conversation started or someone was blocked, never the words.
- **Members under 18** are never in the directory or on the leaderboard, adults never see their
  status or mood, and an adult's first message always waits in their Requests.
- **Not yet:** GIFs (until the GIF library is chosen), stickers, linked organizations (part 24D),
  and collaborators (part 24E).
- The plan, every decision, and what is left are in the
  [Phase 24 checklist](../phases/phase-24-checklist.md). **Leave this conversation** deletes it
  from this PC only (ADR-173); the other person keeps their copy.
