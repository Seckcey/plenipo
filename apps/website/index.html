A few good questions.

What does it mean to hire an agent?

You add an AI agent to a role in your organization, with responsibilities, instructions, and permissions. Like staffing a team, you decide what the role is there to do and who it reports to. A supervisor coordinates the work; the connected AI model performs it.

How does Plenipo choose a model and effort level?

Automatic agents follow model policies. Plenipo supplies starting role preferences, such as preferring another provider for code review. You can adjust choices for the organization, department, role, or individual agent, or fix an agent to a particular tool and model. Selection considers your rules and tool availability, and records why a model was chosen. Supported effort levels follow your settings, then model or tool defaults.

Is Plenipo free?

Yes. The current release is free and unrestricted. Free and Pro editions are planned for a future release, with Pro planned at $9/month or $99/year. Your AI tools use your own accounts; their subscriptions and usage limits are separate.

Does Plenipo run entirely offline?

No. Plenipo runs on your PC and keeps its permissions, approvals, and activity record locally. AI requests go to the AI companies you choose, so supported cloud tools need an internet connection.

Which computers are supported?

The current installer supports Windows 11 on x64 PCs. Check the latest release for available downloads and release notes.

What can a worker do without approval?

A worker can do the things allowed by its role's permissions and project folder limits. Actions set to ask wait for your approval; blocked actions stay blocked. Optional settings can allow sending, buying, or signing in without asking on allowed websites. These settings start off. You can review requests, revoke permissions, take over, or stop work from the app.

Can I read and change the source?

Yes. Plenipo is source-available under the Elastic License 2.0. You can read the source, build it, and change it for your own use, subject to the license terms.
