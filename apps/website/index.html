A few good questions.

Is Plenipo free?

Yes. The current release is free and unrestricted. Free and Pro editions are planned for a future release, and Pro pricing has not been announced. Your AI tools use your own accounts; their subscriptions and usage limits are separate.

Does Plenipo run entirely offline?

No. Plenipo runs on your PC and keeps its permissions, approvals, and activity record locally. AI requests go to the AI companies you choose, so supported cloud tools need an internet connection.

Which computers are supported?

The current installer supports Windows 11 on x64 PCs. Check the latest release for available downloads and release notes.

What can a worker do without approval?

A worker can do the things allowed by its role's permissions and project folder limits. Sensitive actions wait for your approval; blocked actions stay blocked. You can review requests, revoke permissions, take over, or stop work from the app.

Can I read and change the source?

Yes. Plenipo is source-available under the Elastic License 2.0. You can read the source, build it, and change it for your own use, subject to the license terms.
