# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a small project about getting work done — treat everyone here the way you would
treat a colleague you respect.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and direct. Critique the code, not the person.
- Assume the other person is acting in good faith, and give them the benefit of the doubt.
- Accept feedback on your own work gracefully, and give it the same way.
- Keep it on topic. Issues and discussions are about Plenipo.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, including anything about someone's race, sex, gender identity
  or expression, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, age, religion, or national origin.
- Sexualized language or advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing a thread.

## Scope

This applies everywhere the project happens: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages,
and any other space where you are representing Plenipo.

## Reporting

If someone is behaving this way, report it privately through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/Seckcey/plenipo/security/advisories/new) or by
messaging a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile. Do not post the report as a public
issue.

Reports stay confidential. The maintainers will look into it and decide what to do, which may
include a warning, deleting comments, or blocking someone from the repository. The maintainers'
decision is final.
